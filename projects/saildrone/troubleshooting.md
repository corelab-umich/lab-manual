## Record a resolved issue

- **Symptom:** observed behavior and exact error message.
- **Scope:** affected hardware revision and software version.
- **Context:** operating mode and the last known working configuration.
- **Diagnosis:** checks performed and evidence.
- **Resolution:** confirmed change and verification result.
- **Provenance:** author, date, and related experiment or issue.

Keep unresolved hypotheses labeled as such. Archive obsolete fixes with their original version context rather than presenting them as current guidance.
