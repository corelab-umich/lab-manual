# Home

The shared documentation repository for **Control and Optimization for Renewables and Energy Efficiency (CORE) Lab** at the University of Michigan.

Preserve the information needed to reproduce a build, understand a configuration, and continue an experiment after its original author leaves the lab.

The SP-48 section includes recovered SN11 information files and controller documentation. The kite and Saildrone sections are initial scaffolds. Hardware-specific procedures require review against the current configuration.

## Start here

1. Read [Getting started]({{ '/getting-started/' | relative_url }}).
2. Recover existing notes with the [migration and recovery guide]({{ '/recovery/' | relative_url }}).
3. Follow [Contributing]({{ '/contributing/' | relative_url }}) to write or review a page.
4. Record the build revision and experiment context with the [templates]({{ '/templates/' | relative_url }}).

## How to read a page

**Draft** means incomplete or unverified. **Reviewed** means a named reviewer has checked a specific revision. **Archived** means retained for historical reference. A review applies only to the hardware and software versions stated on that page.
